Three honest outcomes.

We don't manufacture certainty just to give you an answer.

🟢 VERIFIED

Independent evidence supports the important claims. That doesn't mean risk is impossible—it means the key facts we checked are supported.

🟠 UNVERIFIED

We couldn't independently establish the claim. This is not automatically a scam. It means you should not rely on the claim yet.

🔴 CONTRADICTED

Independent evidence conflicts with an important claim. We explain exactly what conflicts and why it matters.

How TrustLens investigates.

We start with a simple question: what would have to be true for this to be legitimate?

01

Extract

People, organisations, URLs, phone numbers, amounts, dates and requests.

02

03

Verify

Compare claims with independent evidence and look for contradictions.

04

Explain

Give you the result, the evidence and a practical next step.

Our standard.

TrustLens is built around one principle:

No evidence → no confidence.

AI can help investigate and reason about evidence. It should never be presented as proof simply because an AI model sounds confident.
